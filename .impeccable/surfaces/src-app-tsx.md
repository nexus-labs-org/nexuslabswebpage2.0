Scope: the NEXUS LABS home, a single page (src/App.tsx). Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport), with a Persuade close (start a project).

Audience: founders/product teams at startups and established companies choosing a digital product studio. Job: see the range of work fast, understand what Nexus builds, start a conversation. Action: "Começar um projeto" (contact form, e-mail).

Constraints: user pinned labs.griflan.com as the structural reference (explorable curved grid of project clusters, boxed header, side panels, ticker); Griflan's copy, mascot, imagery and colours are not used. Project images are illustrative placeholders, swapped later from src/projects.ts and public/projects/. No invented clients or metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the studio's work as one territory you pan across, not a portfolio of cards. Refuses the hero-headline-plus-card-grid agency page.

OWN-WORLD: Keystone night. Deep Slate ground #0F172A, hairline slate grid, Crisp White type, Luminous Cyan #00E5FF as the single action block, Cerulean only in the mark. Manrope for UI, Instrument Serif for the two header taglines, JetBrains Mono only for measured readouts (coordinates, ticker). Square corners, 1px rules, boxed header cells.

STORY: the visitor sees dozens of digital products at once, understands "apps, platforms and systems", opens one, then starts a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: boxed two-row header (Sobre/Projetos | mark + tagline | wordmark centre | tagline | Contato, cyan "Começar um projeto" block); below, the full-bleed barrel-distorted WebGL grid with 3x2 project clusters; coordinate readouts top corners; ticker pill bottom centre.

FORM: direction pinned by the user (labs.griflan.com structure); the concept roll was deliberately skipped because a pinned direction beats the roll. seed: none (pinned by user, roll skipped)

Signature interaction: drag/flick the grid with momentum; hover lights one cluster; click opens its project drawer. Motion grammar: one entrance (lens settles from zoomed to rest), drawers on ease-drawer, everything else still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
